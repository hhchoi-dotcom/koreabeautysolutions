Who We Are

A Passion for Authentic Korean Beauty

PT Korea Beauty Solutions (KBS) was founded with a single mission: to bring authentic, high-quality Korean beauty products to Indonesia. We work directly with Korean manufacturers and brand owners to ensure every product we carry meets the highest standards of quality and authenticity.

Our team combines deep expertise in the Korean beauty industry with on-the-ground knowledge of the Indonesian market — giving our brand partners and customers the best of both worlds.

Based in Tendean, Jakarta Selatan, we serve both individual consumers and business partners across Indonesia through a growing network of salons, retailers, and e-commerce channels.
